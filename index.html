FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions employees ask about PF withdrawal, claim rejection and EPFO issues.

How long does PF withdrawal take?
Online PF withdrawal typically takes 7 to 20 working days once the claim is submitted correctly. The timeline depends on KYC completion, Aadhaar seeding, employer exit date update and claim type. We help ensure all pre-conditions are met so your claim is processed without delays.
Can a PF overlap issue be solved?
Yes, PF overlap issues can be resolved. An overlap occurs when two employers contribute to PF for the same period. We coordinate with the concerned employers and EPFO field office to merge or correct the records. The process may take 30–60 days depending on employer cooperation.
What if my employer is not updating my exit date?
You can file a grievance on the EPFO grievance portal and also approach the Regional PF Commissioner. We handle this process on your behalf — drafting and submitting the complaint, following up with EPFO, and ensuring your exit date is updated so your withdrawal is not blocked.
Can rejected PF claims be corrected and resubmitted?
Absolutely. Most claim rejections happen due to KYC mismatches, incomplete documents or incorrect form details. We identify the exact reason for rejection, fix the issue (name correction, Aadhaar seeding, bank linking) and resubmit the claim with correct documentation to ensure approval.
Is online PF assistance available?
Yes, 100% online support is available. You do not need to visit our office. Simply share your issue and documents via WhatsApp and our team handles everything remotely. We serve employees across all states in India through our online support system.
Which documents are required for PF withdrawal?
For PF withdrawal, you typically need: UAN number, Aadhaar card (seeded to UAN), PAN card (for withdrawals above ₹50,000), active bank account (linked to UAN), and exit date updated by employer. For specific cases like pension withdrawal or advance withdrawal, additional forms like Form 10C or Form 31 may be needed. We guide you through the exact documents needed for your specific case.
